How this works & the honest truth

Can it predict the next spin? ›
Not on a fair wheel. Every spin is independent — past results don't change the next one (that belief is the gambler's fallacy). The only real, data-driven edge is detecting a physically biased wheel, and that needs thousands of spins on the same machine. The colour/odd-even suggestion only means something when this app shows "Bias detected".
Magnets & rigging (Switzerland) ›
  • Licensed casinos (Swiss ESBK, tested by eCOGRA/TST) essentially never use magnets — getting caught means losing the licence, and the house edge already wins long-term.
  • Magnet cheating shows up in unlicensed venues, or from players with magnetic balls.
  • What you can actually catch is mechanical bias — loose frets, tilt, wear — which makes one sector run hot. The Analysis tab is built for exactly this.
  • Whether the cause is wear or tampering, if the ball favours a physical zone, the sector test will surface it.
Casino design psychology ›
  • Maze layouts, no clocks or windows, warm light — you lose track of time.
  • Near-miss effect and win jingles trigger the reward system and FOMO.
  • The results board is shown on purpose to make you hunt for patterns that don't exist on a fair wheel.
  • The green zero(s) make the long-run expectation negative — the longer you play, the closer you get to that loss.
